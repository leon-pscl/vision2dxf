Capture a session

A research prototype. Its measurements have not been checked against standardised manual anthropometry, and must not be read as equivalent to a tape measure or used for any medical purpose.

1 — Subject and consent

A code, never a name: the name is not stored anywhere.

Measured by the operator, shoes off. Not the subject's own estimate.

Stature converts pixels to millimetres. It is a calibration input only, and is not evidence that any other measurement is accurate.

The profile decides which fields must be present before a pattern-ready export is allowed.

2 — Before you capture

  • Fitted clothing: no thick layers, no loose fabric.
  • Shoes off, standing flat on the floor.
  • Hair tied flat or under a tight cap: loose hair sets the head row too high.
  • An uncluttered background, with nobody else in frame.
  • A-pose, arms 20–45° from the body and not touching it; the same posture for every view.
  • The camera stays where it is, at the same zoom, for every view.

3 — Camera

Each shot gives you three seconds to hold still; between views there is longer to turn.

4 — Views

0 of 3 accepted Front is required. At least one side view is required.

5 — Process

Processing finds the landmarks, takes the measurements and writes the provenance report. You review and correct everything afterwards.
