1 — Subject

Measured by tailor, shoes off. Do not use the client's own estimate.

Every measurement is scaled by this height, so a wrong number scales the whole body.

2 — Camera

Stand inside the guide, facing the camera, arms 20–45° from the body.

  • Shoes off, standing flat on the floor.
  • Hair tied flat, or a tight cap: loose hair sets the head row too high.
  • Fitted clothing, no thick layers or loose fabric.
  • A-pose, arms 20–45° away from the body, not touching it.

3 — Captures

Front is required. At least one side view is required. No camera? Use a stored sample, or upload a photo.
